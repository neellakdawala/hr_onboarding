# IT Setup Guide for New Hires

**Department: IT**
**Last updated: January 2026**

## Getting your work laptop

Your work laptop is delivered to your desk on your first day. If you are
working remotely, the laptop is shipped to the address on file 3 to 5
business days before your start date. Contact IT support if it does not
arrive by end of day one.

## Setting up your account

Your company email and Single Sign-On (SSO) account are provisioned
automatically 24 hours before your start date. Your temporary password
is sent to your personal email. You will be required to change it on
first login and enable two-factor authentication.

## VPN access

VPN access is required when working from any non-office network. Install
the GlobalConnect VPN client from the IT self-service portal. Use your
SSO credentials to log in. If your VPN client fails to authenticate,
raise a ticket with IT support.

## Getting hardware or software

Standard hardware (monitor, keyboard, mouse, headset) is available on
request through the IT self-service portal. Software installations
outside the pre-approved list require manager approval and a ticket
to IT.

## Common issues

If you forget your password, use the "Forgot password" link on the SSO
login page. If your account is locked, contact IT support directly.
For hardware issues, raise a ticket with a description and photograph
if applicable.

## IT support

IT support is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm local time.
Urgent issues outside these hours should be raised through the on-call
support channel.
