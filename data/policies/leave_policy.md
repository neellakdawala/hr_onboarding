# Leave Policy

**Department: HR**
**Last updated: January 2026**

## Annual leave

Every full-time employee receives 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave accrues monthly and is available from the date of joining. Unused annual
leave may be carried over into the following year, up to a maximum of 5 days.
Any unused days beyond that will be forfeited on 31 December.

## Sick leave

Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical
certificate is required for any sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.
Sick leave does not carry over into the following year.

## Unpaid leave

Employees may request unpaid leave in exceptional circumstances. Requests
must be submitted at least 14 days in advance and are subject to manager
and HR approval. Unpaid leave does not affect annual leave balance.

## Maternity and paternity leave

Employees are entitled to 16 weeks of paid maternity leave and 4 weeks of
paid paternity leave. Requests must be submitted at least 30 days before
the expected start date.

## How to apply

All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal. Requests
require manager approval before they are considered active. Half-day
leave is supported for annual and sick leave categories.

## Approval workflow

Leave requests are automatically routed to the employee's direct manager
for approval. Requests longer than 5 consecutive days additionally require
approval from the department head. HR reserves the right to review any
leave request in accordance with company policy.
