# Security Policy

**Department: Security**
**Last updated: January 2026**

## Password requirements

All accounts must use a password of at least 12 characters, containing
uppercase and lowercase letters, at least one number, and at least one
special character. Passwords must be changed every 90 days. Reusing any
of the last 5 passwords is not permitted.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is mandatory for all company accounts.
Use the company-approved authenticator app. SMS-based 2FA is not
permitted for accounts with access to sensitive systems.

## Handling company data

Company data must not be stored on personal devices or personal cloud
storage. Approved storage locations are the company OneDrive, SharePoint,
and the internal file server. Sharing links must be restricted to
authorized recipients only.

## Reporting security incidents

Any suspected security incident (phishing email, lost device, suspicious
activity) must be reported to the Security team within 4 hours of
discovery. Do not attempt to investigate on your own.

## Acceptable use

Company devices are for business use. Limited personal use is permitted
but must not interfere with work responsibilities or violate any
company policy.

## Access reviews

Access to systems is reviewed quarterly. Employees are responsible for
requesting access removal for systems they no longer need.
